### About

MSc **Data Science** at [Warsaw University of Technology](https://www.mini.pw.edu.pl) (BSc with honors). I work at the intersection of **NLP**, **explainability**, and **practical data science** — shipping research as installable packages with docs, not just notebooks.

Previously built [Botuj](https://botuj.pl) (AI chat + voice support); no longer actively developing it.

Roles & experience → [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pawel-pozorski)

**What I care about**
- Shapley-value XAI for text / audio multimodal models
- AutoML & class-imbalance methodology you can reproduce
- Small, sharp tools around local LLM workflows
